# Third-party assets

## hand-holding-phone.png

A photograph of a hand holding a phone with a blank screen, used in the hero so
the app is shown being held rather than floating.

- Title: *Female Hand Holding Smartphone With Blank Screen*
- Author: personalgraphic.official
- Source: https://www.flickr.com/photos/198895458@N04/53097224746
- Licence: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) — public
  domain. No attribution is required; this file records the provenance anyway,
  so a later reader can tell where it came from and that it is ours to publish.
- Found through the Openverse index (https://openverse.org).

Changed from the original: the white studio background was keyed out to
transparency by flood-filling from the edges, and the image was resized to
760x760. The blank screen it was shot with is where the app is composited, which
is what a blank-screen mockup is for.

Everything else under `assets/` is our own.
