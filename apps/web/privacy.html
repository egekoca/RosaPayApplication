Rosa Pay

Privacy Policy

What the app stores, what it never sees, and who else receives data when you use it. Last updated 13 September 2026.

Rosa Pay currently runs on the Stellar test network. Balances are test tokens with no monetary value, and no real funds can be moved. This policy describes the app as it behaves today.

The short version

  • Your wallet's private key is created inside your device's secure hardware and never leaves it. We cannot read it, copy it, or recover it.
  • We run no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs. There is no third-party profiling of your behaviour in the app.
  • Payment records we store are public blockchain facts plus the request that produced them.
  • If you use the Turkish lira transfer feature, your bank details go directly to the anchor that moves the money — they never reach our servers.

What we store

Your wallet

The public address of your account, its network, and the public half of the key that signs on your device. Public keys and addresses are, by design, already visible to anyone on the Stellar ledger.

Business profile, if you accept payments

The display name you choose, the Stellar address you want to be paid at, and — optionally — an email address you provide. You are not asked for a business registration, tax number, or identity document.

Payments

For each payment request: the amount, the asset, the merchant and recipient addresses, a reference line, an expiry, and the signatures that authorize it. Once settled, we also store the transaction hash, the ledger number and the outcome. Nearly all of this is published on the Stellar ledger by the payment itself.

Sessions

To keep one phone from acting for another, the app proves possession of its key and receives a short-lived session. We store the public signer, a single-use challenge, and expiry timestamps.

Operational records

A log of what happened to each payment, for support and reconciliation. It is built to refuse anything that names a secret, and authorization and signature fields are redacted from our logs.

What we never receive

  • Private keys or recovery phrases. They stay in the device's secure hardware, or in your own keeping.
  • Biometric data. Face ID and fingerprint checks happen on your device; the app only learns whether the check passed.
  • Bank account numbers and IBANs. When you deposit or withdraw Turkish lira, those details are sent from your phone to the anchor providing that service. They do not pass through our servers.
  • Contacts, photos, or location. The app does not request them.

Camera and NFC

The camera is used only to read a payment QR code, and what it reads is processed on your device. NFC, where available, is used only to exchange the same payment request with a nearby phone. Neither produces images or recordings that are sent anywhere or stored.

Who else receives data

  • The Stellar network. A payment is a public transaction. Its addresses, amount, and timing are permanently visible to anyone, and we cannot delete or alter them.
  • The lira anchor. If you use the deposit or withdrawal feature, the anchor receives the identity and bank details it requires to move the money, under its own privacy policy.
  • Our hosting and database providers, strictly to run the service.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

What is on the ledger cannot be deleted

You can ask us to delete the records we hold — your business profile, the email you gave us, and the server-side history of your payments. What is already written to the Stellar ledger is outside anyone's control, including ours, and will remain public.

Signing out of the app deletes the device identity and session from your phone. The wallet itself lives on the ledger and survives.

Children

Rosa Pay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top of this page changes with it, and the new version is published here before it takes effect.
