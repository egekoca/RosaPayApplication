The idea inside the name

Lumen meets lemonade.

Lumens (XLM) are Stellar's native currency. Lumenade pairs that foundation with the clarity and freshness of lemonade—the same combination expressed by the Stellar mark inside our lemon.

LUMEN+LEMONADE=LUMENADE
One app, both sides of the counter

A payment experience people already understand.

Merchants ask for the exact amount. Customers see exactly what they are approving. Stellar handles the settlement underneath.

01
⌗

For customers

Scan a QR on either platform—or tap on supported Android devices—review the merchant and amount, then approve with the device security you already use.

  • Exact amount and recipient before approval
  • No seed phrase in JavaScript or on a server
  • Final receipt with a Stellar transaction hash
02
▦

For merchants

Activate merchant mode in the same app, create a signed payment request, and follow it from presentation through confirmed settlement.

  • Signed, expiring, single-use payment intents
  • QR everywhere with optional Android NFC
  • Request status and verifiable receipts
Verifiable by design

Three independent approvals.
One exact payment.

01
M

Merchant signs

The request binds merchant, recipient, asset, amount, nonce, network and expiry.

02
C

Customer authorizes

The phone verifies the request and only asks the device signer to approve the values on screen.

03
R

Relayer submits

The relayer simulates, sponsors and submits, but cannot change the authorized payment.

04
S

Stellar settles

The contract enforces asset policy, expiry, exact values and replay protection before transfer.
